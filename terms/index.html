Terms of use

Updated 6 September 2026. Five short terms cover everything this site does. Buying a piece adds one more: the invoice at the counter.

1. This site is a window, not a till

Prices, descriptions and availability shown here are an invitation to inquire, not an offer to sell. The sale happens in person at 143 Wooster Street or by invoice. Prices are in USD and the holiday code LUMIERE15 (15% off) applies at the counter or on the invoice, not to third-party payment links we don’t have.

2. Reservations

When you reserve a piece, we wrap it and hold it at the atelier for 5 business days, free. If life intervenes, one email or call releases it back to the tray; we never hold it over your silence longer than that, and we never charge you for changing your mind.

3. Care & the lifetime promise

Unlimited cleaning, re-plating and minor repairs are ours to provide for as long as you own the piece, as described on the Care & Service page. Damage from trauma (a lost stone from a hitting, a chain worn by a lawnmower) is quoted before we touch it. Nothing here limits your statutory rights as a New York consumer or anyone else’s.

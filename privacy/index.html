Privacy policy

Updated 6 September 2026. This page describes what this site actually does, because it is a small one and the honest version fits here.

Who we are

Lumière Jewels, 143 Wooster Street, New York, NY 10012. For anything on this page, write to hello@lumierejewels.com or call (212) 555-0143.

What we collect, and why

When you submit an inquiry or join the Inner Circle, the form sends us: your name, email, optionally a phone number, your message, the topic, and (for inquiries) any pieces on your bag list plus campaign link parameters if you arrived from one. That is the entire collection. We use it to answer you, prepare a viewing or a care visit, and, if you ticked the box, to write about private viewings. Legal basis: your consent and, where needed, the steps you ask us to take before buying.

What we do not do

No advertising networks, no session recording, no email beyond what you ask for. This site sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts by default. Site analytics are switched off unless you click “Accept” on the notice at the bottom of your first visit, and even then they count pages, not people. Your saved bag never leaves your browser: it sits in this device’s storage until you send it or clear it.

Hosting & forms

The site is hosted on Netlify. Form submissions are processed by Netlify Forms, which briefly logs the request (including IP address and user agent) for spam protection before delivering the message to us. We use their honeypot, not fingerprinting.

Retention

Inquiry threads are kept while we are working with you and for 3 years after, which is how long lifetime-promise paperwork can matter. Newsletter lists live until you unsubscribe, one click.

Your rights

Ask us and you can see, correct, export or delete anything we hold on you; we’ll do it within 30 days and without making you fill out anything. If analytics consent is on, you can switch it off anytime from the cookie notice on this browser. You can also complain to the FTC or the New York Attorney General, though we’d rather you just emailed us first.

Children

This shop is for adults; we don’t knowingly take information from anyone under 13, and a viewing inquiry from a child gets returned to a parent, politely.

Changes

If this page changes materially, the date above changes and the footer of this site says so. No surprises, no buried updates.

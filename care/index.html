Care & Service

Five minutes a week, a lifetime of brightness

Jewelry is the one thing you wear through everything. Here is how to keep it looking new, and exactly what we owe you when it doesn’t. Last updated 6 Sep 2026.

The weekly habit

  1. Wipe, don’t scrub

    Once a week, ten seconds with the polishing cloth from your linen box. It carries the right abrasive for vermeil and rhodium; paper towels do not.

  2. Perfume first, jewelry last

    Lotion, spray and sunscreen go on before a piece does. Chlorine and hot springs are the real killers; leave rings at home for those.

  3. Store it dry and alone

    Back in the linen box, chain fastened. Pearls are the exception: let them breathe, no plastic pouch.

  4. Bring it in when it feels loose

    A stone that ticks or a clasp that softens is a same-week visit, not a funeral. We catch it early and it stays free under the promise.

Lifetime promise

Your piece, forever

“Every Lumière piece includes unlimited cleaning, re-plating, and minor repairs. Because beautiful things deserve to stay beautiful.”

In plain terms: bring any Lumière piece to the atelier, any year, and we clean it, re-plate the silver or vermeil, and fix small wear (tightening a stone, easing a hinge, re-tipping a clasp) at no charge, for as long as you own it. Lost stones, resized shanks and full rebuilds we’ll quote first, honestly.

  1. Measure a ring that already fits

    Lay it flat and measure straight across the inside, wall to wall, in millimeters. Match the table above and you are done; that inner diameter is all the bench needs.

  2. No ring to copy?

    Wrap a strip of paper round the finger, mark where it overlaps, measure the strip in millimeters and send us that number in your inquiry. We convert it, and Inner Circle members get our paper sizer in the post for next time.

  3. Between sizes, think season

    Fingers run about a quarter size larger in August than January. Take the larger if you hesitate, and remember the first adjustment within year one is on us either way.

Five questions we get at the counter

How should I care for my Lumière piece?

The weekly habit above is the whole answer: wipe with the cloth, keep perfume and chlorine off it, store it dry, and let us know the moment a stone feels loose.

What does the lifetime promise cover?

Unlimited cleaning, re-plating and minor repairs, on us, for as long as you own the piece. Anything that needs new metal or a new stone gets an honest quote before we touch it. Full terms →

Where do your stones and metals come from?

Recycled 14k gold and conflict-free, ethically sourced diamonds, with full supply chain transparency. Solitaires are VS clarity and arrive certified; our sterling is rhodium-plated for lasting brilliance. The sourcing ledger →

Can I try pieces before buying?

Yes. The SoHo studio doubles as a private showroom. Try everything in natural light, with a stylist, Tuesday to Saturday 11am–7pm, or Sunday by appointment. Hours and directions →

Do you offer engraving?

On signet faces and flat surfaces, cut by hand at the bench. Ask at pickup or mention it in your inquiry and we’ll quote it for the piece.

Our philosophy

Designed to be lived in

Each piece begins as a hand-drawn sketch in our SoHo atelier. We work exclusively with recycled 14k gold and ethically sourced diamonds. Our sterling silver is rhodium-plated for lasting brilliance.

Lifetime care, complimentary. Every Lumière piece includes unlimited cleaning, re-plating and minor repairs, because beautiful things deserve to stay beautiful.

Materials, on the record

What our pieces are actually made of

14k gold
Fully recycled, used solid in the Étoile pendant and as vermeil over sterling on the pavé pieces.
Diamonds
Conflict-free and ethically sourced. Solitaires are VS clarity and ship with their certificate.
Sterling silver
Rhodium-plated by hand for lasting brilliance, and re-plated free for life if it ever dims.
Pearls
8–9 mm baroque freshwater pearls, chosen one by one, so no two drops sit alike.
Chains
Woven in Italy by fourth-generation silversmiths in Arezzo, then finished at our bench.

Full supply chain transparency is part of how we work. Ask who cut your stone and we’ll tell you. More on craft & materials →

Sent insured, returned without drama

A return, step by step

  1. Thirty full days

    Count from delivery or pickup, not from checkout. Wear the piece around the house, see it in your own light, then decide. Bought in November or December? You have until 15 January.

  2. Unworn outside, box included

    The piece comes back as it left, in its linen box with the cloth. A quick bench check, and the refund goes back the same way the money arrived: cash, card slip or invoice reversal, usually within 3 business days.

  3. Two honest exceptions

    Engraved pieces and shanks cut beyond the stock 4 to 10 range are final sale; we will say so before either happens. Everything else, no questions worth rehearsing.

  4. Starting one is one email

    Write to hello@lumierejewels.com or call (212) 555-0143. We send an insured label, you drop it at any post office. Return shipping is on us.

Resizing & fit

Ring sizes 4 to 10 are stocked, and the first adjustment at the bench is free in year one. After that, or beyond the stock range, we quote before we cut. Not sure of a size? The size tables on the care page sort it in two minutes with a ruler, and Inner Circle members get our paper sizer in the post.

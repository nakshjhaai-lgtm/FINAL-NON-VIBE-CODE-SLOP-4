What a visit looks like

  1. A tray, not a counter

    Say what you came for and the tray that matches is on the table: the pavé bands, the pearls, everything for a first look.

  2. Daylight, deliberately

    The showroom faces Wooster Street so you see pieces in the light you actually live in, not a spotlight trick.

  3. Watch the bench

    The polishing wheel is ten steps away. If someone is setting stones, you’ll see the burnisher move. That’s also where your engraving happens.

The people, by trade

We’re a small house that doesn’t do headshots: the designer who draws the first sketch, a setter who works only hands-on with stones under two millimeters, a polisher with twenty years on the wheel, and a stylist who runs the showroom floor. Fourth-generation chain makers in Arezzo, Italy, count as family for this purpose.

Good to know

Same-day pickup runs through this door: order by 2pm, collect by 6pm. Repairs and re-plating under the lifetime promise are handed over at the same counter. There is no commission structure here; nobody’s paycheck depends on what you buy.
